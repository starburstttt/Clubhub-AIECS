# Stakeholders · ITC Club Hub (v0.1, 2026-10-20)

| Stakeholder              | Interest                                      | Influence | Needs from Club Hub                                           | How we reach them                         |
|--------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|-----------|---------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| ★ Students               | Find events and get a seat without hassle     | Medium    | Browse events, register, cancel, get a reminder               | Short survey, club chat groups            |
| ★ Club leaders           | Run events without counting replies by hand   | High      | Register the club, publish events, see who registered         | Interview 2-3 leaders                     |
| ★ Client (TA)            | A project that meets the course requirements  | High      | Clear scope, a working C++ core, honest risk analysis         | Lab sessions, PR review on GitHub         |
| Organisers               | Smooth check-in on the day                    | Medium    | Check registered students in, see attendance                  | Ask club leaders to introduce them        |
| Administrators           | Oversight of clubs and their activity         | High      | Approve clubs, see a monthly activity report                  | Request a short meeting through the TA    |
| Institute IT staff       | Security and protection of student data       | High      | No leaked personal data, no extra load on their systems       | Email through the TA                      |
| Our team (KoPrei)        | Finish a working project and pass the course  | High      | Clear tasks, a shared codebase, enough time                   | Telegram group, weekly Scrum meeting      |
| Instructor               | Students learn software engineering practice  | High      | Evidence of process: history, reviews, documents              | Lab sessions, course site                 |
| Future maintainers       | A codebase they can understand and extend     | Low       | Documentation, tests, readable code                           | README and docs in the repository         |
